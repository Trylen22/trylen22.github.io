The Hunt Project

Louisiana Tech works with Hunt Forest Products at the LaSalle Lumber sawmill in Urania, Louisiana. The goal is to reduce unplanned downtime and trim loss with data the mill already collects.

The mill's telemetry, work orders, and vibration surveys live in separate systems that do not share identifiers. I build the models and tools that join them: a process model of every machine center, live signal views on the production dashboard, downtime-to-work-order attribution, and an AI-assisted analysis loop that hunts for rules an operator can act on at 3 AM.
